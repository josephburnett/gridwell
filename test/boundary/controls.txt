# Every control the page takes a press on, the one list of them. A row is a
# control: its name, how it is brought on screen, what a press on it does, and
# whether a finger and a mouse do the same (same, or differs: with the
# CLAUDE.md decision that says how). sites are the press installs that serve
# it, as TestEveryControlIsARow spells them from client/wasm and
# web/index.html: every install is some row's, and every site a row names is
# installed. web-every-control.spec.ts presses every row with a finger at a
# phone's size and with a mouse at a desktop's, and asserts what the row says
# a press does and that the client is still running. A listener that hears
# every press for bookkeeping is a row too, its devices "not a control: <why>",
# and has no case. The canvas is one element and draws many controls; the
# rows here press the ones a notice or the bar's circle draws, and the device
# axis (client/touchgest/deviceaxis_test.go) holds the canvas's pure verdicts.
#
